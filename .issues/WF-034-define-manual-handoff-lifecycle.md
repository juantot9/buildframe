## Question

What exact local commitment, destination evidence, durable records, user-visible material, success boundary, cancellation, reveal or copy failure, repetition, redirection, restart, recovery, and downstream Workflow behavior define a v1 Manual Handoff when no correlated Harness Capability is selected—including whether a live Attached Harness is required—without creating an Invocation or claiming delivery, start, execution, enforcement, outcome, or semantic correctness?

## Blocked by

- [Test the blueprint for completeness](./WF-017-test-blueprint-completeness.md)

## Resolution comment

Manual Handoff is one immutable, destination-scoped local commitment, not a fourth Handoff Route and not a degraded Invocation. It preserves the same route-neutral Workflow Handoff meaning and authority that a correlated path would carry, but its only Neoflow-controlled effect is making exact committed material available to the user. Effect Constraint is `none`; even where the material expresses discussion or bounded mutation authority, Neoflow cannot claim that authority was communicated, received, enforced, or acted upon.

A live Attached Harness is not required. Every Manual Handoff instead freezes a **Manual Handoff Destination** containing the intended Pi or Claude Code harness, the owning Workspace Identity and canonical checkout, any user-recognizable session label, and the provenance and verification state of each destination fact. A currently validated Attachment Identity may seed and strengthen that evidence, but attachment, bridge reachability, and capability Availability remain optional and confer no correlation. With no attachment, the interface says the session is user-declared or unverified rather than inventing identity. Clipboard, terminal pane, file, or TUI view is a presentation channel, never the destination. A known checkout mismatch cannot be approved as if it were the owning workspace; the user must target that workspace or move the Workflow through the already-defined import and Workspace Rebinding path.

The strongest compatible correlated Capability remains the default. Manual Handoff may be the prominent action when no correlated option exists because the integration is absent, the Handoff is unsupported, or only an uncorrelated envelope exists. A temporary correlated-route blocker does not by itself invalidate an otherwise exact Manual Handoff, but the limitation remains visible. Selecting Manual Handoff while a stronger compatible option is available is an explicit Capability Downgrade naming the lost destination validation, Effect Constraint, correlation, lifecycle evidence, and interruption behavior. Lack of a correlated option never overrides a workflow-semantic blocker: unknown or incoherent material, failed durability, invalid authority, stale or unverified Review evidence, unresolved approval prerequisites, or another missing truth required by the Handoff still blocks commitment.

Before commitment, the TUI presents one compact action with the declared destination, exact workflow scope and authority, and the plain-language limitation **“You will carry this manually; Neoflow cannot know what happens after.”** Progressive detail exposes the destination evidence and verification limits, exact Artifacts, Batches, context captures, exclusions and unavailable items, sensitivity, intended workspace, and the absence of correlation and enforcement. Mutation authority, sensitive disclosure, a stronger declined route, destination change, or known overlapping activity composes into the existing single gate. The action remains explicitly named `Show manual handoff` or its workflow-specific equivalent, never generic `Continue`, `Send`, or `Start`.

One Durable Commit establishes the immutable Workflow Handoff, Manual Handoff Destination, exact versioned human-carriable rendering and component manifest, and Commitment Receipt before any post-commit reveal or clipboard write. The rendering is built only from the accepted immutable Handoff payload; it records exact bytes, ordering, boundaries, provenance, counts, and every metadata-only, unsupported, unavailable, sensitive, or unrepresentable item. It never re-resolves a locator, consults current workspace state, silently truncates or omits content, copies ambient conversation, or lets an Adapter rewrite the material. The exact semantic contents of each Handoff kind remain for [Define v1 Workflow Handoff payload contracts](./WF-031-define-v1-workflow-handoff-payloads.md); Manual Handoff adds presentation and truth boundaries rather than a second payload model.

The Commitment Receipt references the exact Handoff, destination, authority, consent and any downgrade, durable time, and the manual capability basis. It contains no fabricated Capability Snapshot when none was selected. If a viable correlated Capability was deliberately declined, the receipt may reference that inspected Capability evidence only to explain the downgrade, never as a guarantee used by the Manual Handoff. It also contains no Invocation Identity, Preparation, Harness Offer or Decision, start or terminal evidence, Interruption Route, transcript, clipboard contents, or mutable delivery status.

The semantic success boundary is the Durable Commit: the exact Manual Handoff now exists and is **Ready to carry**. Local presentation remains a separate fact. A successful reveal proves only that Neoflow displayed the exact committed material; a successful copy proves only that the host clipboard interface accepted those exact bytes. The history uses `Ready to carry`, `Shown`, `Copied locally`, or an exact local failure—not `Sent`, `Delivered`, `Awaiting start`, `Active`, `Done`, or `Outcome unknown`. External use is always explicitly unknown and is not represented as unresolved Harness activity.

If durability or exact rendering fails before commitment, no Manual Handoff, receipt, reveal, or copy occurs and the prior authoritative state remains unchanged. If reveal or copy fails after commitment, the immutable commitment is not rolled back and no Harness Interaction Failure is created; the interface reports the local failure and lets the user reopen and retry the exact saved bytes. A crash between a clipboard side effect and its durable local acknowledgment may leave that presentation attempt locally unknown, but it still creates no Invocation and says nothing about Harness use. Material is never regenerated from newer workspace or Artifact state to recover a presentation failure.

Cancelling or leaving the preview before Durable Commit creates no Manual Handoff. After commitment, dismissing the material or abandoning the transfer cannot withdraw, delete, or mark the immutable decision unused; the user may reopen it later. Workflow Cancellation archives the Workflow and preserves the Handoff, material, destination and receipt, but cannot revoke material the user may already have carried. It neither requests interruption nor creates an unresolved state. Restart performs no automatic reveal, clipboard restoration, resend, or inference: committed material reopens exactly, incomplete pre-commit work does not exist, and corrupt material follows normal Recovery Quarantine rather than being reconstructed.

Reopening, inspecting, or retrying reveal or copy of the same committed material is local recovery and creates no new Handoff. An explicit attempt to carry the work again—even to the same declared destination—or any change of harness, workspace, or session creates a new Manual Handoff identity, destination-scoped commitment, and Commitment Receipt linked to the earlier one. Because Neoflow cannot know whether the earlier material was used, the composed gate names duplicate or overlapping execution risk; there is no automatic retry and no Explicit Resubmission because no prior Invocation exists. A duplicate handling of the same commit command remains idempotent and resolves to the original commitment.

Manual Handoff occupies no Attachment single-flight slot and never changes an existing Preparation, Offer, Invocation, or their evidence. Known awaiting-start, active, or unresolved activity at the intended destination remains visible and uses the existing overlap guardrail, but Manual Handoff cannot clear, settle, or correlate it. Attachment loss or later capability change never rewrites the committed destination evidence; choosing a different current destination is redirection and requires a new commitment.

Downstream workflows may rely only on the local facts that the exact Workflow Handoff was committed and its material made available for manual transfer. They do not advance automatically on reveal or copy. Independent human decisions—Re-review dispositions, Plan Approval or withdrawal, Review completion, Workflow Cancellation, or another workflow-specific action—may proceed under their own prerequisites, but none retroactively proves manual delivery or Harness activity. A later Plan Harness Offer, generated Artifact, workspace change, or correlated Invocation remains new evidence requiring its own explicit association; matching content or timing does not correlate it with the Manual Handoff. Later decisions may reference the Handoff, its approved Plan Revision, Review Batch, Continuation Brief, or Implementation Baseline while continuing to label external execution and outcome as unverified.

Canonical language is sharpened in [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md), and the conceptual Module Interface and manual commit outcome are reconciled in [`docs/blueprint/harness-capability-seam.md`](../docs/blueprint/harness-capability-seam.md).
