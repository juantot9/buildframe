## Question

How should v1 Home organize durable Workflows and attention; what exact relationship, eligibility, visibility, and reopening or inspection behavior distinguish Workflow Cancellation, completion, Archive, unarchive, and recoverable Trash; and how should Workflow Resume choose and explain the most relevant semantic return point when focused Artifacts, drafts, open Annotations, pending commitments, awaiting-start, active or unresolved Invocations, stale or unverified evidence, recovery needs, completed passes, unavailable attachments, and cancelled, archived, or trashed Workflows compete—without restoring ephemeral interface or Harness state or inventing tasks?

## Resolution comment

Home is a calm workspace entry surface with three jobs: start deliberate new work, surface durable conditions that genuinely need the developer, and return to retained work. It is not a lifecycle board, recency feed, Harness activity dashboard, or storage browser. The header keeps `New plan` and `New review` directly visible rather than hiding them behind a generic workflow picker; these are primary actions beside, not a third list competing with, **Needs you** and **Continue**. A compact Workspace and connection summary links to Connections & Diagnostics when relevant, but routine healthy integration detail stays out of the way. When no Workflow exists, the same two actions lead the empty state.

**Needs you** is an evidence-derived projection rather than an inbox with unread badges. It includes only a required human decision, a recovery or integrity problem, an external outcome whose uncertainty affects safe next work, a result ready for human Re-review, or a truth-critical change that would otherwise be hidden. A merely recent Workflow, durable draft, active Harness turn, completed pass, unavailable optional integration, or stale historical Artifact does not become attention by itself. Stale or unverified evidence appears only when it blocks or materially qualifies an available next action; unavailable attachment appears only when a selected or committed route needs that exact destination.

Attention is ordered by consequence: live deadline-bound decisions and commitment blockers first; Recovery Quarantine, unresolved delivery or Invocation evidence, and identity or integrity failures next; response-ready Artifacts, Re-review and disposition work, and significant late evidence next; then other action-blocking conditions. Recency breaks ties only within the same consequence class. One Workflow contributes one compact highest-priority row, with additional conditions disclosed inside it, so a single troubled Workflow cannot flood Home. Each row says what changed, what Neoflow knows, why the developer is needed, and one accurately named action; it never translates evidence into an inferred task or urgency claim.

**Continue** contains every non-archived, non-trashed Workflow, including completed Workflows the user has deliberately kept visible. Each compact row leads with the user’s goal, Planning or Reviewing identity, plain-language lifecycle position, last meaningful durable milestone, and one explained Resume action. Route, Attachment, draft, finding, or warning detail appears only when it changes that action. Awaiting-start and active activity can be visible status without appearing under Needs you. Dense technical evidence remains one level deeper. This preserves the settled Editorial Ledger hierarchy: compact scanning around a calm Artifact-first destination, sparse framing, redundant textual status, and semantic reflow on narrow terminals.

Workflow Resume chooses one deterministic return point from current durable semantic state. A currently required decision, blocker, or recovery condition wins; otherwise an awaiting-start, active, failed, rejected, or unresolved external attempt returns to its exact Handoff and evidence surface; otherwise response-ready Re-review, open disposition-requiring Annotations, or a late Artifact wins; then a durable draft; then the Workflow’s explicit focused Artifact; and finally its latest Completion, Review Result, or lifecycle summary. Within one class, the current Review Pass or active Planning lineage wins, then explicit durable Artifact focus, then the most recent durable occurrence. Historical staleness, optional attachment loss, and unrelated evidence never displace more relevant current work.

The Resume control is named for the destination—such as `Review 3 updated findings`, `Inspect unresolved Pi run`, `Continue plan draft`, or `View completed review`—and gives a short reason like `Pi stopped reporting after the review started`. It may focus an exact Artifact, Annotation, decision, Handoff, Invocation, recovery report, or completion record, but performs no refresh, delivery, replay, attachment selection, lifecycle transition, or external effect. It restores no pane, cursor, scroll, editor Presence, Harness conversation, stream, or model state. Process restart recomputes the same projection from Authoritative Workflow State rather than persisting a UI route.

Planning Completion and Review completion are semantic outcomes, not organization. Completion never archives a Workflow. A completed Planning row may inspect the exact approved Plan, begin an eligible Implementation Handoff, start a linked Review from an exact committed Handoff, or explicitly reopen Planning. A completed Review row opens its exact Review Result and may explicitly start a successor Review Pass with the unchanged Change Set. Viewing, inspection, beginning an already-eligible Handoff, and starting a linked Review do not reopen Planning; no completed state silently becomes active work.

Archive is only an organizational visibility state. Archiving removes a Workflow from Continue while preserving every lifecycle fact, Artifact, relationship, receipt, and evidence stream. Archive inspection is read-only with respect to new workflow work and does not unarchive. `Unarchive` restores ordinary Home visibility but neither reopens, restarts, refreshes, attaches, nor resubmits anything. When the user chooses a legitimate action that requires visible active work, Neoflow may compose the organizational transition into one precise control such as `Unarchive and reopen planning`; the Durable Commits remain distinct facts even though the interface avoids ceremony.

Archive cannot hide a truth-critical condition. An archived or cancelled Workflow may still contribute a Needs you row for a live Native Gate decision, unresolved or newly correlated external evidence, Recovery Quarantine, a late contract-valid Artifact, or another consequential condition. The row is clearly labelled `Archived` or `Cancelled` and first permits exact inspection. Resolving it does not silently unarchive or reopen the Workflow. Routine history, completed outcomes, drafts, and merely old warnings remain in Archive rather than leaking back into Home.

Workflow Cancellation is a semantic decision to end current work and archive the complete Workflow; it is not Completion, ordinary Archive, deletion, or proof of Harness interruption. It preserves drafts, Artifacts, decisions, Batches, Handoffs, Invocations, receipts, and later valid evidence. Inspecting a cancelled Workflow does not reactivate it, and unarchive alone is insufficient. Cancelled Planning returns to revision work only through explicit Planning Reopening, composed with unarchive when needed, and requires fresh Approval under the settled lifecycle. A cancelled Review is never resumed as if cancellation had not happened: `Start review again` creates the already-defined new Review and relationship while preserving the cancelled Review. Active or unresolved external effects retain their existing cancellation preflight, interruption, evidence, and overlap rules.

Recoverable Trash is a separate Workspace Storage concern and never appears in Needs you or Continue. `Delete to Trash` moves the whole Workflow and its dependency-preserving record into that holding state without rewriting Completion, Cancellation, Archive, Approval, Handoff, Invocation, or evidence. Known live Offers, open Preparations, pending decisions, and awaiting-start or active Invocations must first cross their existing decision, withdrawal, expiry, cancellation, or interruption boundary; Trash cannot be used to hide or imply cancellation of live work. An unresolved Invocation may remain unresolved after a cancelled Workflow is trashed, with that consequence visible on the direct delete action and in Workspace Storage rather than behind a second generic confirmation.

A trashed Workflow remains exactly and read-only inspectable from Workspace Storage. `Restore` exits Trash and restores its exact prior organizational and semantic state; it does not unarchive, reopen, refresh, reattach, or replay anything. A linked retained Workflow continues to inspect dependency Artifacts preserved by a trashed source, while navigation offers exact source restoration rather than a dangling link. Permanent purge remains governed by Empty Trash’s Destructive Consent Gate and transitive dependency closure: Neoflow includes dependents in the exact scope or blocks the purge. Valid evidence may still append to preserved records, but Trash intentionally remains outside Home until restoration.

Attention and Resume remain capability-honest. An unavailable Attachment never causes automatic attachment, destination substitution, Manual Handoff, or replay; Resume opens the blocked action with the shortest truthful correction or alternative. Failed and unresolved routes retain exact evidence and duplicate or overlap guardrails. Completed, cancelled, archived, and restored Workflows never manufacture success, work items, Harness attribution, Plan compliance, or semantic continuation.

No prototype is needed because the accepted attention-and-continuity boundary determines the smaller interaction consequences while remaining consistent with the validated terminal and visual systems. No new ticket or fog is surfaced. Canonical Home, Workflow Attention, Workflow Resume, and Workflow Archive language is sharpened in [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md); final blueprint retesting remains in its existing ticket.

### Post-dogfood amendment — direct Reviewing entry and maintenance-free history (2026-07-30)

The first Reviewing dogfood gate exposed that presenting several equally prominent Review rows before the user could inspect current changes required them to understand Neoflow's workflow history too early. For the Reviewing entry path, an active Review now opens directly. With no active Review, the launcher presents one selected `Review current changes` action and one plain-language sentence. Escape returns from a Review to that minimal launcher without cancelling or changing durable meaning.

Completed and cancelled Reviews no longer compete in ordinary entry or continuation. A secondary `Past reviews` action opens a newest-first read-only list whose rows contain only outcome date/time, Completed or Cancelled status, changed-file count, and Question count. It requires no titles, folders, tags, branches, manual Archive, or routine cleanup. Opening a past Review never reactivates it, and Escape returns through Past reviews to the launcher.

An exact current-change inspection with zero changed files reports `No current changes to review` and remains on the launcher. It creates no Review, Review Pass, Review Snapshot, Review Result, cancellation, or Past-review row. For a Review with actual changed files, the human may neutrally `Finish review`; the Reviewing owner derives a no-recorded-findings Result only when no findings exist. The interface never asks the human to assert “no findings,” and leaving the surface does not finish it.

`Review other changes` is a secondary launcher action, not another primary dashboard row. It opens one bounded local commit browser over history reachable from `HEAD`, with topology plus branch/tag decorations. **Commit** mode means selected parent→commit; **Range to HEAD** means selected commit→`HEAD`. `n` jumps to the visible `HEAD~N` starting commit, `/` resolves a local hash, branch, or tag, and multi-parent commits require explicit `h/l` parent selection. The graph makes those semantics visible through one compact selection rail in its left margin rather than repeated labels. A filled point is included, a hollow point is the basis, and bracket corners group the endpoints: Commit mode brackets one filled commit to its hollow parent; Range mode brackets filled visible commits in the exact local revision set to the hollow start. An empty start-at-HEAD range is one hollow diamond. The ASCII form preserves the same `+`, `|`, `*`, and `o` rail. Preview still names Parent/Commit or Start/Head on separate lines for exact identity confirmation. Full immutable content capture occurs once, only when Enter starts the Review; all earlier preview uses endpoint and changed-file metadata only. It never fetches or changes the checkout. The resulting exact Snapshot uses the ordinary Review, Question, Result, and history flow.

Past Review history is governed by [Review Retention](./WF-010-set-persistence-portability-recovery-policy.md): eligible old whole Reviews expire automatically without ordinary prompts or list tombstones. This narrowly supersedes the prior requirement that every completed Review remain in Continue until manual organization; full Planning and truth-critical Home attention semantics remain unchanged. The accepted single-flow prototype replaced three cognitively demanding dashboard variants before production implementation.

### Post-dogfood amendment — Current and Previous Reviews (2026-08-03)

Reviewing no longer has active/completed continuation as its ordinary organization. Startup opens the one Current Review when present. Escape returns to the minimal launcher without lifecycle meaning. `Start new Review` captures the selected comparison and atomically changes the Current Review pointer; the former Current Review becomes Previous without completion, cancellation, archive, deletion, or judgment. Previous Reviews remain in a newest-first retained list with their Snapshot, Questions, captured Responses, and Harness evidence and open read-only for inspection. Historical completed/cancelled dogfood Reviews remain labelled by their committed facts. A zero-change start still records nothing and leaves the existing Current pointer unchanged.
