## Amendment (2026-09-08)

[Reframe Reviewing around the current repository](./WF-225-reframe-reviewing-around-the-current-repository.md) and its confirmed child map narrow this contract's Reviewing half: Reviewing no longer curates, captures, or transmits a supporting-context basis per Handoff. Its outgoing material is one small message (text + `file:line`; plus exact labelled historical lines for saved-origin messages), and additional repository reading is the Harness's own business. The curation/capture/representation contract above remains fully in force for **Planning** and for any future case that deliberately re-introduces curated context. Change-driven review-tree bases (WF-226) reuse exact-capture mechanics where a saved point exists but do not capture the live basis per message.

## Question

How should Planning and Reviewing curate, preview, capture, refresh, exclude, and disclose workflow context across TUI selections, Context Locators, Editor Context Contributions, prior Artifacts, and Review supporting context—including source eligibility, directory expansion, duplicate or overlapping selections, ignored, generated, binary, missing, changed, unsaved, outside-checkout, sensitive, oversized, and unrenderable content—so every snapshot and Handoff payload is exact and failures degrade explicitly?

## Resolution comment

Planning and Reviewing use one context-curation contract while preserving their different Artifact lifecycles. Before capture, each workflow owns a mutable **Workflow Context Selection** assembled from TUI Context Locators, immutable Editor Context Contributions, exact prior-Artifact references, and explicit include and exclude rules. The selection is private curation state rather than an Artifact, payload, or consent grant. A mutable locator denotes current inclusion intent; an Editor contribution or Artifact denotes one already-immutable occurrence. General cross-workflow Context Reuse remains post-v1, while the already-required approved-Plan supporting context in Plan-to-Review Continuity uses this same exact-source contract.

Context Locators remain live only while curation is open. Resolving a file or bounded directory produces a stable candidate manifest and preview captures rather than turning the path into payload. A new descendant of an included directory enters the proposed selection and appears as an addition. A changed file proposes a successor capture of its newest coherently observed content and shows a difference from the previewed capture. A deleted file remains visible as missing and is never silently replaced by cached bytes. In either case the user may accept the proposal, exclude the source, or deliberately pin the previous exact capture as historical context. Refresh never rewrites a capture. Editor Context Contributions, including unsaved buffers, and prior Artifacts never refresh automatically; selecting newer editor content or another Artifact creates a distinct contribution.

Context Source Eligibility, exact capture availability, and Handoff representation are independent. Ordinary in-checkout directory expansion includes tracked files and non-ignored untracked files; a tracked file remains eligible even when an ignore rule matches it. Ignored untracked content is excluded by default but may be explicitly included as a bounded sensitive source. An outside-checkout file, range, or bounded directory is eligible only through explicit selection and is sensitive. Generated content is neither guessed nor excluded: explicit repository evidence or user classification may label it, but selection still controls inclusion. Directory expansion never dereferences symlinks, enters submodules or nested repositories, escapes through a symlink, or implicitly broadens beyond its selected root. Symlink, Gitlink, large-file indirection, sparse, missing-object, and other filesystem evidence follow [Define the workspace and change-set evidence contract](./WF-023-define-workspace-change-set-evidence.md) rather than inventing a second source model.

Every resolved selection has a complete manifest independent of byte capture and rendering. Each candidate records its source kind, lossless identity and path, selected range or whole-source scope, source version or Artifact Identity, size, type and availability, provenance, inclusion origin, current/changed/missing state, and sensitivity markers. Exact locally admitted bytes are immutable and self-contained. Binary content is never lossy-decoded; generated, binary, oversized, or unrenderable content remains navigable in the manifest with exact captured-byte and presentation status. A chosen Handoff Route may represent exact bytes, exact bounded ranges, metadata only, or no content for an item, but it may not silently truncate, summarize, decode, substitute, or omit it. The preview names every representation and limitation.

Directory and file limits are named, measured, and versioned rather than hidden heuristics. Candidate discovery may render progressively and defer content reads, but it never presents a truncated expansion as complete. Per-item, aggregate-capture, and route-payload limits report exact counts and sizes and preserve the full known manifest. When complete membership is known but bytes are unavailable or beyond a capture limit, the item degrades independently with its exact reason. When Neoflow cannot establish a complete directory expansion or coherent selected-source state, the proposed snapshot and Handoff are blocked until the user narrows, excludes, retries, or deliberately uses a prior pinned capture; unknown scope cannot become an acknowledged omission.

Selection rules make refresh predictable. Excluding a file keeps that path excluded across content changes and recreation; excluding a directory creates a prefix exclusion for present and future descendants. A rename is a missing old path plus a new candidate and never carries an exclusion by inferred identity. A more-specific direct inclusion deliberately overrides an inherited directory or ignore exclusion and exposes any resulting sensitive status. Equal-specificity actions replace rather than coexist as contradictory rules. The interface preserves user source-group order and uses stable lossless path order within an expansion.

Overlap is normalized without erasing provenance. The same exact source occurrence selected through multiple locators is carried once while retaining every inclusion origin. Overlapping ranges from that occurrence are represented by their exact union, and whole-file inclusion subsumes its ranges for payload bytes. Content equality alone never deduplicates different paths, Artifact Identities, source versions, old and new Review sides, or saved and unsaved evidence. If a disk locator and an Editor Context Contribution cover nominally the same file but the buffer differs, both remain distinct and the preview exposes the conflict for exclusion or deliberate retention.

Capture is coherent rather than a sequence of unrelated reads. Neoflow resolves mutable selectors, records the selected manifest and exact bytes, and revalidates every included source identity, membership fact, range, mode, size, and digest using the evidence rules already established for workspace capture. Relevant movement receives only a small bounded retry. Stable additions, changes, deletions, or unavailability return to the disclosure delta; continuing movement publishes no mixed-time successor and preserves the previous previews and Artifacts. Timestamps alone never establish equality.

In Planning, context remains fluid until `Start exploration`. That action revalidates the accepted selection and, when no material delta or exceptional acknowledgment is pending, atomically Durable-Commits one self-contained Planning Context Snapshot and its exact Workflow Handoff without another confirmation. The Snapshot freezes the goal, admitted captures, resolved locator expansion, explicit and default exclusions, unavailable-item reasons, ordering, provenance, and sensitivity evidence. If revalidation finds added, refreshed, expanded, newly sensitive, or no-longer-representable material, one composed preview appears before commitment; a further change invalidates that preview rather than racing it. Later workspace or editor changes never rewrite the Snapshot. Planning has no in-place snapshot refresh after exploration starts; later divergence is Context Drift or requires another explicitly defined workflow action, not ambient context synchronization.

In Reviewing, selected supporting context is frozen inside each Review Snapshot while the Change Set evidence remains governed separately by [Define the workspace and change-set evidence contract](./WF-023-define-workspace-change-set-evidence.md). A change to an included mutable locator’s resolved membership, identity, content, or availability makes that Snapshot stale; changes under an explicit or default exclusion do not. An immutable Editor contribution or Artifact does not become stale merely because its original external source changes. Explicit refresh re-runs the same selection rules, presents the exact delta and Re-anchor consequences, and creates a successor Review Snapshot only when its captures or resolved manifest differ; an unchanged refresh reuses the existing Snapshot. Unavailable selected context may remain preserved for diagnosis, but an unverified Review Snapshot cannot support Review Batch commitment until the source is captured, pinned exactly, or excluded through a verified successor.

Every Handoff Disclosure Preview is calculated from the immutable proposed snapshot and the exact Attachment Identity, Capability Snapshot, and Handoff Route. It shows the destination; included source groups and stable order; each capture’s path or Artifact label, range, size, provenance, and sensitivity; exact Context Budget Evidence; and every metadata-only, unavailable, excluded, over-limit, binary, or unrepresentable item. Its Handoff Disclosure Delta is measured against the captures deliberately curated and acknowledged for that destination. Additions, refreshed bytes, expanded ranges, changed representation, destination changes, and newly detected sensitivity are individually inspectable and excludable through one composed gate. Matching already-curated captures need no second acknowledgment, while unsaved, ignored, outside-checkout, credential-like, or advisory secret-flagged captures retain destination-scoped Sensitive Disclosure Acknowledgment. Detection is advisory and absence of a marker never claims safety.

The context portion of a committed Handoff is exactly the accepted route projection of that snapshot; an Adapter cannot re-resolve locators or add ambient workspace, editor Presence, transcript, raw diagnostics, or newer Artifact content. Route-specific payload structure remains for [Define v1 Workflow Handoff payload contracts](./WF-031-define-v1-workflow-handoff-payloads.md), but it must preserve these source identities, exact inclusions, explicit degradations, and disclosure counts. Failure to Durable-Commit the snapshot or build that known projection blocks external contact and preserves prior state. A known route limitation may proceed only through its existing composed consent or Capability Downgrade semantics; unknown content, incoherent capture, or silent omission remains non-overridable.
